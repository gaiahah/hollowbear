The Hollow Bear sits outside time and roams woods far from the ones we know. I lie inside its belly and rarely am conscious enough to remember the past. Moss grows on its fractured ribs and retains memories or dreams of those who came before. Here I share the ones sticking to the back of my head. Heavy paws tread on young weeds.
